## How to sit

- Use a mat or a platform to sit. Do not sit on the floor directly.
- Sit facing East

## Items Required
- Yagnopaveetam (Janivara, Janeu)
- Kumkuma (Vermillion, Sindoor)
- Haridra (Turmeric, Arishina)
- A mixture of Rice and Sesame
- A plate containing two fruits, betel leaves, Dakshine, Yagnopavetam (Optional; For Dana)

## Before starting

- Apply Turmeric to the Yagnopaveta followed by Vermillion.
- Make sure you have put vermillion on your forehead.
- Make sure to select the appropriate japa count below when you reach the guide. The mantra will change accordingly.

## Japa Count
Default is 108. If you can do it, chant it 108 times. Otherwise, select the appropriate count, or if you want to do more, you can use the Yatha Sambhava option. It will loop continuously until you stop. There is a counter for you to know how many times you have chanted so far.
