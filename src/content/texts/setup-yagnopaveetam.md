## How to sit

- Use a mat or a platform to sit. Do not sit on the floor directly.
- Sit facing East

## Items Required
- Yagnopaveetam (Janivara, Janeu)
- Kumkuma (Vermillion, Sindoor)
- Haridra (Turmeric, Arishina)
- A plate containing two fruits, betel leaves, Dakshine, Yagnopavetam (Optional; For Dana)

## Before starting

- Apply Turmeric to the Yagnopaveta followed by Vermillion.
- Make sure you have put vermillion on your forehead.
